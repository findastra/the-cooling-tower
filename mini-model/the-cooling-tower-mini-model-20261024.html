The night, in order

  1. INBreak in. Spawn in the yard, grab a hard hat and clipboard, slip through the cut fence and duck between the X-legs.
  2. 1The Stack. Main floor on the drained basin, crowd all round the DJ booth on the water riser. Lasers and smoke up to the sky.
  3. 2Reactor Hall. Down the stairs and through the water pipe. Second booth on the refuelling bridge over the glowing pool.
  4. ↺Pad back to 1. A glowing pad by the pool returns you to the stack mid-set.

One DJ live at a time; both floors hear the same set. The turbine hall on the way is an optional third floor.

Who runs the lights

Each DJ booth has its own lighting panel, built into the decks (the amber consoles labelled in the view). The live booth has the lights:

  • Riser booth (1) runs the Stack’s rig: lasers, moving heads, wash ring, smoke, beacons.
  • Bridge booth (2) runs the Reactor Hall’s rig: pool glow, crane lights, and the Stack’s rig too while it is live, since both floors hear its set.
  • When the set moves booths, the lights follow. Under the panel, the Light Show Generator keeps the show running from the music by itself; the panel is for steering: colour, intensity, strobe, smoke burst, blackout, breakdown.

  • 16 moving heads on the balcony ring
