The night, in order

  1. INBreak in. Spawn in the yard, grab a hard hat and clipboard from the crate, slip through the cut fence and duck between the X-legs.
  2. 1The Stack. Up the stairs to the grating deck above the X-legs, crowd all round the low DJ stage on the water riser. Lasers and smoke up to the sky; rain falls in the rain zone underneath now and then.
  3. 2Turbine Hall. Walk through the giant water pipe into the hall. An arched high bay with a crane; the floor runs alongside the turbine train on its pedestal, with booth 2 on a low stage in front of it.
  4. 3Reactor Hall. Through the equipment hatch into the dome. Booth on the refuelling bridge over the glowing pool, crowd ringing it.
  5. ↺Pad back to 1 whenever you want.

All three are joined: the pipe runs straight from the stack into the turbine hall, and the hatch opens from the hall into the dome.

Inside the reactor hall

The reactor is the steel vessel under the pool, beneath the bridge: lid off, core glowing blue at the bottom, 8 m of water above it. Hover over or tap any part to see its name.

Who runs the lights

Every venue has a DJ booth with a lighting panel in its decks. One DJ is live at a time. The live booth runs the lights in its own venue and every venue the night has already passed: booth 1 runs 1, booth 2 runs 1 and 2, booth 3 runs all three. Venues still ahead stay dark until the set reaches them. Under the panel, the Light Show Generator keeps the show running from the music.
