# The Cooling Tower

> **This is a draft.** The world is still being designed; nothing is built in Unity yet.
>
> - **Website:** https://findastra.github.io/the-cooling-tower/
> - **Mini Model** (the whole world as a little 3D model you can spin on a phone, no VR needed): https://findastra.github.io/the-cooling-tower/mini-model/the-cooling-tower-mini-model-20261024.html

a VRChat rave world inside a nuclear plant's cooling tower: a crew in hard hats breaks in during a refuelling outage, dances in the dry stack under lasers and smoke, walks the giant water pipe into the turbine hall, then goes through the hatch into the glowing reactor hall.

## The night: three connected venues

1. **The Stack:** the big floor on the drained basin, crowd all round the DJ booth on the water riser in the middle, a balcony ring above.
2. **Turbine Hall:** the water pipe leads straight in. The floor is beside three turbines, with booth 2 on the turbine deck.
3. **Reactor Hall:** through the equipment hatch into the dome. Booth 3 on the refuelling bridge over the glowing pool. A pad takes you back to 1.

One DJ is live at a time. Each booth has its own lighting panel, and the live booth runs the lights in its own venue and every venue the night has already passed (booth 2 runs 1 and 2, booth 3 runs all three), on top of the automatic Light Show Generator.
