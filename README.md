# The Cooling Tower

a VRChat rave world inside a nuclear plant's cooling tower: dance on the grating where the steam normally rises, under coloured smoke and a ring of sky, then climb down the water tunnel to the reactor hall, where the opened reactor glows blue under the DJ.

## Status

Seeded 2026-10-24: design and research first, no Unity project yet.

- [Design and plant research](docs/design-20261024.md): what a real cooling tower and reactor building look like, how the world maps onto them, and the tools needed to build it.

## Working in it

- When the Unity project is made, it starts fresh from VRChat Creator Companion's World template (Unity 2022.3.22f1), with nothing copied from another world. A piece from another world comes in only when Astra names it.
- Uploads, releases and `main` change only with Astra's OK.
