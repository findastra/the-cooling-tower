# The Cooling Tower

> **This is a draft.** The world is still being designed; nothing is built in Unity yet.
>
> - **Website:** https://findastra.github.io/the-cooling-tower/
> - **Mini Model** (the whole world as a little 3D model you can spin on a phone, no VR needed): https://findastra.github.io/the-cooling-tower/mini-model/the-cooling-tower-mini-model-20261024.html

a VRChat rave world inside a nuclear plant's cooling tower: a crew in hard hats breaks in during a refuelling outage, dances in the dry stack under lasers and smoke, then walks the water pipe to a second booth on the bridge over the glowing reactor.

## The night

1. **The Stack:** the main floor on the drained basin, crowd all round the DJ booth on the water riser in the middle.
2. **Reactor Hall:** through the water pipe, a second booth on the refuelling bridge over the glowing pool. A pad takes you back to 1.

One DJ is live at a time and both floors hear the same set. Each booth has its own lighting panel; the live booth runs the lights, on top of the automatic Light Show Generator.
