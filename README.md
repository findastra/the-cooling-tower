> **The world is still a draft.** It is being designed; nothing is built in Unity yet.

a VRChat rave world inside a nuclear plant's cooling tower: a crew in hard hats breaks in during a refuelling outage, dances in the dry stack under lasers and smoke, walks the giant water pipe into the turbine hall, then goes through the hatch into the glowing reactor hall.

## The night: three connected venues

0. **Break in:** run along three giant pipes through a cut in the barbed-wire fence, through one cut panel of the inlet netting, and down a scaffold stair into the dry basin.
1. **The Stack:** the grating deck above the X-legs, crowd all round the round DJ stage on the water riser; lasers and LED-lit smoke pour out of the top, rain falls underneath, and a graffiti-tagged ladder leads to the rim where people sit and watch.
2. **Turbine Hall:** the walk-through water pipe leads straight in. An arched high bay with the turbine train on its pedestal; booth 2 is a glass-fronted control room on the floor, its alarm windows flashing on the beat.
3. **Reactor Hall:** up the scaffold stair through the equipment hatch into the dome. Booth 3 is a round stage on the refuelling bridge over the open, glowing reactor. A pad takes you back to 1.

One DJ is live at a time. Each booth has its own lighting panel, and the live booth runs the lights in its own venue and every venue the night has already passed (booth 2 runs 1 and 2, booth 3 runs all three), on top of the automatic Light Show Generator.
