Pipe & Scaffold Spawn

Try the two tools that build the pipes' supports and every scaffold in the Cooling Tower Mini Model. Change a setting and the tool rebuilds and checks itself.

pipe-spawn

Supports at ASME B31.1 spans, one beside every elbow; shared rack bents, T-posts, hangers indoors, sleepers near the floor, truss bridges between raised roofs.

scaffold-spawn

Switchback stair towers and access scaffolds: rise ≤ 0.2 m on a 0.25 m going, a landing every lift, bays ≤ 2.6 m, braced, boarded, guarded.

Source: tools/pipe-spawn-20261101.js and tools/scaffold-spawn-20261101.js. Both also run in Node.
