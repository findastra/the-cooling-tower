## Build scope
Golden photomultiplier sensors on a cylindrical tank; independently purified recirculating water; electronics/readout racks; sensor high-voltage supplies; calibration access; low-radon ventilation; power, data and cooling services. Model UPS and a dry observation deck as world design additions. Place the annex east of control offices with a service corridor; the visible tank represents a cutaway of a deep underground cavern.

## Science and fiction
Neutrino interactions create charged particles whose Cherenkov light is recorded by sensors. The show can visualize sensor hits and expanding rings driven by the music, framed as a fictional calibration sequence. It does not measure quantum entanglement, and music does not power a nuclear reactor. Research water is separate from cooling-tower and reactor circuits.

## Related site decisions
Lake-fed make-up supply; no dam required by the concept. Retain controlled pumping and condenser circulation pumps. Staff parking east of the warehouse, with pedestrian access to control. Emergency egress markers and an assembly point are concept placeholders, pending a full connected circulation review.
